# Limitations and safety

TrekVerse AI is a planning prototype. Do not use it as a substitute for official route, access, weather, medical or emergency guidance.

## Data and location

- The merged app uses curated sample destinations for ten starting cities, not worldwide live discovery.
- Country names are not city origins. Unsupported locations must not receive unrelated fallback trips.
- Travel distances/times, activity grades, costs and access details are illustrative assumptions, not verified routing, quotes or current permissions.
- Images may be representative rather than photographs of the named place.

## Weather, readiness and routes

- No live weather, closure, hazard, rescue or emergency service is connected.
- Readiness is not a safety score or permission to depart.
- The Digital Twin is an accelerated simulation with sample/synthetic terrain and checkpoints; it is not GPS, navigation or verified elevation.
- A rain scenario is a user-selected planning assumption, not a detected forecast. An alternate place is not thereby safe or open.

## Money

- INR, MYR and SGD conversions use a frozen ECB reference via Frankfurter dated **2025-01-02**.
- Reference conversion does not establish a current retail exchange rate or destination price.
- Missing legacy monetary units mean INR, even for international trips. Display currency changes must not silently relabel saved values.

## Collaboration and storage

- Members and votes are edited locally; no invitations, remote group presence or live synchronization are implemented.
- Data belongs to this browser profile and can be lost if its storage is cleared or unavailable.
- No login, cloud backup, cross-device restore, robust cross-tab conflict handling or verified storage-capacity guarantee exists.
- Old record normalization is not a guarantee of recovery from all malformed data.

## Intelligence and impact

- The assistant is rule-based; there is no LLM integration or autonomous external planning agent.
- Quest completion and impact are locally recorded/self-reported, not proof of visiting a trail or independently measured environmental outcomes.
- There are no connected bookings, payments, insurance or safety-clearance products.

## Verification boundary

Documentation and historical screenshots are not current test evidence. The latest native Node logic-test invocation is blocked at module loading. See [testing](TESTING.md) for dates, commands and the distinction between historical passes and current unverified behavior.
